A Letter For You 💌

October 18, 2026
Dear Rumnaz,
How does one put a universe into words when the universe itself has taken the shape of a person? I have tried, you know. I have searched for the loveliest sentences, borrowed the language of poets, and wandered through the pages of novels, hoping to find something beautiful enough to describe you. And yet, every word seems to fall a little short, every metaphor a little pale. Perhaps that is the trouble with loving someone as dearly as I love you!

Happy Birthday, love! My most fav girl, my 10/10 bodmayesh meye! You are the dream my heart holds dear, the sunshine that graces my every day, the dream I scarcely dared to dream, my sweetest reverie, and the loveliest chapter in a story I never wish to end.

My love, I wish I could lend you my eyes for a little while, so you could see yourself the way I see you. Maybe then you would understand why the moon feels a little less lonely when I think of you, why the sunshine feels warmer, and why even the most ordinary moments feel special just because you cross my mind.

I wish I could place the entire universe in your hands, with every star tucked between your fingers, just to show you how much you mean to me. But since I cannot, all I can do is give you my heart and whisper, “Take my hand, take my whole life too, for I can’t help falling in love with you.”

You are in my heart, and you shall be there forever. My greatest wish is that whenever you think of me, you feel as happy as I do whenever I think of you!

Peace, love, blessings, and a very happi birthday, babygurl! ❤️
Your love, Taosif
